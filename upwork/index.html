Common Questions

What kind of work do you take on?

Most of my work is short-form for creators and brands (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) and branded content: interviews, campaigns, podcasts and brand films. I also come from documentary work, which is where I learned to build a story out of raw footage instead of just tightening it.

You're in Brazil. How does that work day to day?

Easily. I work async by default and my hours overlap with most of the US and Canada. You send footage and notes, I send back a cut with timestamps. You get an update at the end of every working day your project is active, even when there's nothing new to report. You will never have to chase me to find out where your video stands.

Can you edit short-form for Reels, TikTok and Shorts?

It's what I'm asked for most. I edit for retention and platform behavior: pacing, captions, sound, aspect ratio, all handled. You just post.

Can you work with footage I already have?

Yes. Most projects start exactly that way. Send your files and a brief, and I'll take it from there. If the footage has problems, I'll tell you what I can fix and what I can't before I start, not after.

How does the revision process work?

I deliver a first cut, you send consolidated feedback in one go, and I adjust. Two rounds are included in every project. Clean and structured, no endless loops.
